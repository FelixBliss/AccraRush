# Prototype Sandboxes

These pages are preserved for isolated testing and comparison. They are not loaded by `index.html` or `3d-prototype.html` and must not be treated as production gameplay.

- `curved-road-graph.html` — curved road, road-relative steering and traffic mechanics sandbox.
- `branch-road-network.html` — branch/junction road network sandbox.
- `combined-curved-driving.html` — combined curved-road driving experiment.
- `curved-road-traffic-environment.html` — alternate curved-road and traffic environment experiment.

Both files were copied from the legacy `accraride/road-network-integration` branch and had visible product branding updated. They have not been certified on Android. Keep them isolated until a test plan passes, then port only verified mechanics into the master game.
